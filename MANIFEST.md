The journal manuscript treats GALIATSATOS as the named method and PLA-BN/TAN as
its first reference estimator. It reports the controlled synthetic experiments
and the strict nested benchmark on real Iris, Wine, and Optical Digits records.
The latter uses training-fold-only semi-synthetic one-vs-rest outcome
definitions; it is not natural multi-study external validation.


Version 0.6.0 adds executed external-cohort and natural-definition clinical validation. Version 0.5.0 added multi-start optimization diagnostics. Existing files under `publication_results/` that predate `convergence_stress_v050.csv` were generated by the v0.4.x experiment series and are retained for provenance; regenerate final manuscript tables with v0.5.0 before submission.
